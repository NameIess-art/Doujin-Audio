# MIT KEMAR impulse responses

Bill Gardner and Keith Martin measured these head-related impulse responses.
Copyright 1994 MIT Media Laboratory. The authors allow free use of the data
with attribution. [Original MIT archive and usage terms](https://sound.media.mit.edu/resources/KEMAR.html)

The WAV files use the diffuse-field equalized responses at zero elevation and
azimuths 30° (front) and 150° (back). The left source mirrors the right source.
The four PCM16 channels represent left source to left ear, left source to right
ear, right source to left ear, and right source to right ear, in that order.
The files use polyphase resampling from 44.1 to 48 kHz. Each stereo pair is
scaled jointly to −2 dB RMS gain for centered pink noise from 150 Hz to 8 kHz.
